Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Creamy pasta combined with tender chicken, Parmesan cheese, garlic, and a rich Alfredo sauce.

Prep time:10 minutes

Cook time:10 minutes

Total time:10 minutes

Servings:10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 8 oz (225 g) fettuccine pasta
  • ¾ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups milk
  • ½ cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large garlic clove, minced
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp garlic salt
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken breasts into small pieces.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  4. Add the chicken and cook until browned and fully cooked.
  5. Remove the chicken and set it aside.
  6. Add minced garlic to the same pan and cook for about 30 seconds.
  7. Add milk and heavy cream.
  8. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
  9. Add the fettuccine.
  10. Cook the pasta until tender, stirring occasionally so that it doesn't stick.
  11. Add the cooked chicken back into the pan.
  12. Gradually add Parmesan cheese while stirring.
  13. Continue stirring until the cheese melts and the sauce becomes creamy.
  14. Season with salt and black pepper.
  15. Garnish with fresh parsley and additional Parmesan.
